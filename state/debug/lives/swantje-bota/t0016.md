# t0016 — the world is silent

## Gap

- Does this world have a phenomenon by which a chance arrangement of ordinary objects can meaningfully echo the form of a specific known object elsewhere — is such patterned resonance or omen-bearing a real feature of how things behave here?
  (Greta stopping dead with an armful of firewood at the sight of Jost and the Orclet-house survivors (bota://books/the-life-of-corda-bota#p22) makes her dropping that wood WITHIN_BOUNDS — the ordinary next beat of a person caught flat-footed. But the claim goes further: it asserts the scattered wood fell into a pattern that echoes the specific chain hung at the forest house's hearth (bota://books/the-life-of-corda-bota#p18), i.e. that this world lets a chance arrangement of objects meaningfully mirror a particular known object elsewhere. That's not weather or incidental set-dressing — it commits to some kind of resonance or omen linking Greta's spilled wood to the house she's just seen two strangers walk out of, and nothing in the record establishes that such patterning is a real phenomenon here, nor what it would mean if it were.)
