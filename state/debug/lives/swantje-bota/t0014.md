# t0014 — the world is silent

## Gap

- Does Corda Bota have a history tied to Alheim Forest from before her present arrival there?
  (A tree standing in the forest is ordinary furniture of an 8km² needle forest whose depths the record admits are largely unwritten (bota://places/alheim-forest#about). But a name cut into its bark that has scarred over long ago pins down a specific proper noun to that place from well before the present: it commits the world to Corda Bota — or the name Corda Bota — having some history at Alheim Forest predating her current arrival. Nothing written establishes any such history. The Narrator's own book (bota://books/the-life-of-corda-bota) opens with her waking alone on the road and being taken by the miller and Aalst as an outright stranger to Alheim (#p1, #p4, #p10) — it never says she has no past here, but it also never gives her one, so whether she (or the name) was ever at that tree before is a fact the world does not yet have.)
