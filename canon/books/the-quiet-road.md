I don't know my own name, so this will have to do for a signature.

I walked the road for what felt like an hour, maybe more — there's no sun in
this fog to tell me otherwise. I kept waiting for something to break the
quiet: water somewhere, a voice, an animal moving in the wet grass. None of
it came. The only sound the whole way was my own footsteps and my own
breath, and the fog seemed to swallow both the moment they left me.

I kept walking because it seemed better than stopping. Eventually I hit
something. I'm not ready to say what.
