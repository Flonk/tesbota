## Text

3E960 — THE GRAIN AT ALHEIM

History of the Blue Uprising does not linger on far granaries. The men who write
of King's Reach, or of the fall of Sesspur, have less to say of Alheim than of a
footnote. And yet it was Alheim's mill, mundane as it stood, that kept the
ration carts moving through the worst of the central plains that year, when the
roads elsewhere had gone to soldiers and to worse — distributing extra rations
to the surrounding country while the Empire's attention lay elsewhere.

Who would have expected a village as benign as Alheim to have such an impact on
the political affairs of the greater empire. Maybe, all the people needed, was
food.
