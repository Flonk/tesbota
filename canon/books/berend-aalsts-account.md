## Text

I gave it to her straight, same as I'd want it given to me.

The boy came same as always, two days back now, midmorning, for a bundle of
offcuts for the inn's fire. He never says much — takes the wood, off he goes.
I didn't watch him down the road, why would I. That's the last I saw of him,
and far as I've heard, the last anyone has.

If he'd come by since, I'd know it. My saw sits within earshot of that door
from dawn to dark, and it hasn't been quiet enough for me to miss him.

— B. Aalst
