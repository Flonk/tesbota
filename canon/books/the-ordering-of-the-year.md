## Text

A day is twenty-four hours. An hour is sixty minutes.

Seven days make a week. Four weeks make a month. Eight months make a year. There
are therefore twenty-eight days in every month and two hundred and twenty-four in
every year, and no month is longer or shorter than another.

The days of the week, in order, are Firstday, Millday, Waterday, Midweek,
Marketday, Restday and Lastday. Every month begins on a Firstday and ends on a
Lastday, and this does not vary.

The months of the year, in order, are:

  Frostfall     the cold comes down
  Deepfrost     the cold holds
  Frostbreak    the thaw
  Seedwake      the sowing
  Longlight     the growing
  Highsun       the height of the year
  Reaptide      the harvest
  Emberwane     the fading

Years are counted from the beginning of the era in which they fall, and the era is
named before the year.

This is the ordering of the year. It does not shift, and no reckoning that
contradicts it is correct, however old the hand that wrote it.
