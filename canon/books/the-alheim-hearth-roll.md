## Text

Kept by Ansel Krohn, reeve of Alheim, for the reckoning of hearth-tax owed at
the crossing toll-house.

Six hearths stand in Alheim this year, as they stood the year before.

The house with the blue door, up the street from the inn, belongs to Greta
Marsch, who keeps the Alheim Inn. She lives there with her son, Jost, her only
child. No rent is owed on it; it passed to her from her own mother before her.

The other five hearths are reckoned separately, as in past years, and need no
repeating here.

— A. Krohn
