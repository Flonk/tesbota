## Text

ALHEIM

Alheim sits on the Aler where the river runs slow and brown through the
sparsely peopled heart of the central plains. Reaching it costs nothing worse
than a dull afternoon: the road from the crossing runs its last five
kilometers past open wheat and barley strip-fields, past the turning wheel of
[[alheim-mill]] loud over the water, and loses itself among the first low
houses without ceremony — no wall to round, no gate to pass, no toll-keeper to
argue with. A traveller simply arrives.

It is a small, serene, and thoroughly unremarkable place, and few travellers
trouble themselves to visit. Alheim has been a farming village for as long as
anyone has kept count, its wheat and barley feeding markets it will never see.

The mill is the one thing here with a history worth the ink. Its earliest
written record runs back to 3E960, when its stores were opened to distribute
extra rations through the surrounding country during the Blue Uprising.
Thoneus, no friend to modesty, makes much of it in [[the-blue-uprising-3]]:

"Who would have expected a village as benign as Alheim to have such an impact
on the political affairs of the greater empire. Maybe, all the people needed,
was food."

The mill still turns today, grinding flour for the village same as always,
and a small plaque by its entrance — a gift of Emperor Karl III, in gratitude
for the uprising's quieting — has not been dusted in some time.

The Bureau of Statistics' 4E196 census counts sixteen souls in six houses,
which this author does not doubt, having counted the houses himself twice out
of boredom while waiting for a ferryman who never came. Notable enough to
name: the mill, [[the Alheim Inn]], and [[the Aler Bridge]].

The small Alheim sawmill supplies cut wood steadily enough for the village,
but unless the reader means to build something in the Alheim area
specifically — and who does — the sawmill at [[Lower Flotburg]] will serve
better.

As anywhere in the plains, do not roam the streets after dark — a line this
guide repeats in every entry, and stands by in this one particularly, on
account of [[Alheim Forest]] pressing close against the village's northern
fields, home to foxes with more aggression than their size warrants.

ALHEIM FOREST

Needle forest, and a large one — some eight square kilometers by my own rough
pacing along its nearer edge, a figure I would not defend past two decimal
places. Fauna, per what the villagers were willing to volunteer over ale:
the usual plains assortment of brown foxes (the aggressive sort mentioned
above), rats, stinging kyvers, deer, and the small wood golems the older
Alheim residents speak of the way people elsewhere speak of weather —
constantly, and without expecting to be believed.

A footpath leaves the riverbank at [[alheim-mill]] and runs in under the
needles, worn well enough by mill traffic and whoever else has cause to go
that way. Where it leads past the treeline this author cannot say — nobody
has troubled to write it down, and the forest keeps the rest of itself to
itself.
