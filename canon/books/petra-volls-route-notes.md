## Text

ALHEIM — the Aler road

Past the mill the road narrows and the fields drop away, and the first thing
you meet is [[the Alheim Inn]], its sign facing the road so a tired team
knows where to stop before it has to think about it. Everything else in
Alheim is further in, past the inn, where the road gives up being a road and
becomes whatever a street is in a place with six houses. I have watered
horses at the inn's trough more times than I can count and never once had to
go looking for the door.

Keep to this order and you will not waste daylight: mill, inn, village. It
has held for the eleven years I have driven this route and I see no reason
it will not hold for eleven more.
