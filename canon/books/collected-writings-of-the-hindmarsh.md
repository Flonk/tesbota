## Text

ON A LESSER KIND OF ORC

A local hero of the second era describes a particularly interesting
encounter in the depths of the marsh:

"Aen Treyb of Orken, Halb of size."

— so the account names it, and the name has held after a fashion.
Adventurers brave enough to enter the inner [[Hindmarsh]] will come to learn
they are no less aggressive than their grown counterparts, whatever else has
been lost to them. It is assumed that Orclets — as the lesser race described
above has since become known — have evolved over time to become shorter, on
account of dietary constraints in the marsh; other scholars hold that a
curse was bestowed on them by a local god, though little enough is known of
Hindmarsh culture to settle the matter either way.

Orclets are rarely seen outside the Hindmarsh, and very few people have seen
them at all — which is why I can offer no illustration for them here.

— Lanus of Virtu
