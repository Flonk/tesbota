## Text

ALHEIM — the mill

I've watered horses at that wheel-noise longer than I care to count, and the
mill's been Moller land the whole while — Mollers before me, by what the old
sawyer tells it, and Mollers before them. Jorgen has it now, has for a few
years.

Can't say I understand him. He'll weigh your sack, hand back good flour, and
say less in the doing than most men manage doing nothing. I've seen him
stand at that wheel of an evening just watching it go round, like it owes
him an answer. Strange man. Never known him to shortchange a sack, though.

— P. Voll
