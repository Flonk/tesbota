Sela Wick is a peddler who walks the road on foot, crossing it repeatedly in
the course of her trade.

## Attested
- Claims the fog along [[the-road]] never stirs with wind, in any weather,
  on any crossing she's made. (Sela Wick's Road-Notes)
- Claims the road is silent but for a walker's own footsteps — no birds, no
  insects, no water sound. (Sela Wick's Road-Notes)
