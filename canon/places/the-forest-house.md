## Map
Stands in a clearing just off the footpath that runs north from
[[alheim-mill]] into [[Alheim Forest]] — the only structure known to stand
in the forest itself. Its chimney smokes, though no hearth in Alheim's own
reckoning accounts for it; whatever tends that fire lives outside the
village's count entirely. Home, it's supposed, to a lone
[[the-forest-house-occupant|Orclet]] — a lesser, aggressive race otherwise
known only from the far-off Hindmarsh, and unheard of anywhere near Alheim
before now.
