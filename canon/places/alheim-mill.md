## Map

Stands at the edge of [[Alheim]] on the Aler, its wheel turned by the river.

## Attested
- t0006 — Alheim Mill's earliest written record dates to 3E960, when it distributed extra rations to the surrounding country to help quell the Blue Uprising, per [[the-blue-uprising-3]] and [[the-pocket-guide-to-the-greater-plains-4e196]].
- t0006 — The mill still operates today, grinding flour for the village, per [[the-pocket-guide-to-the-greater-plains-4e196]].
- t0006 — A plaque gifted by Emperor Karl III stands by the mill's entrance, in gratitude for its part in quieting the uprising, per [[the-pocket-guide-to-the-greater-plains-4e196]].
