## Map

Stands at the edge of [[Alheim]] on the Aler, its wheel turned by the river.
Held by the [[Jorgen Moller|Moller family]] for generations; the current
miller is [[Jorgen Moller]]. A well-trodden footpath leaves the riverbank
here and runs north into [[Alheim Forest]].

## Attested
- lore — Alheim Mill has been Moller land for generations and is currently run by Jorgen Moller, per [[petra-voll-on-the-mill]].
- lore — A well-trodden footpath leaves the riverbank at Alheim Mill and runs into Alheim Forest, per [[the-pocket-guide-to-the-greater-plains-4e196]].
