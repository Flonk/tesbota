## Map
A needle forest of roughly 8 square kilometers, pressing close against
[[Alheim]]'s northern fields. A well-trodden footpath leaves the riverbank
at [[alheim-mill]] and runs in under the trees; little else of the forest
has made it into written record. Off that path stands [[the-forest-house|a
lone house]], its chimney smoking though nothing in Alheim's own hearth-count
accounts for it.

## Attested
- lore — Alheim Forest is a needle forest of roughly 8 square kilometers, home to brown foxes, rats, stinging kyvers, deer, and small wood golems, poorly explored on written record and reached by a well-trodden footpath from Alheim Mill, per [[the-pocket-guide-to-the-greater-plains-4e196]].
